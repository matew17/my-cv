## About

<!-- COPY: about -->
I'm a hands-on, Staff-level software engineer building and modernizing production software—and the engineering systems used to deliver it.

My work spans desktop-to-web migrations, frontend platform architecture, enterprise products, and now Agentic AI and AI-native delivery. The common thread is modernization: recovering how existing systems work, shaping architecture, and helping teams deliver reliable software.

At SoftServe, I designed and built Payworks' agentic engineering harness from scratch around Claude Code. The workflow recovers business rules, constraints, and expected behavior from legacy code, creates tests before implementation, and verifies modernized software. Engineers retain responsibility for supervision, business clarification, technical decisions, PR review, and approval.

I lead six developers while remaining hands-on, collaborating with the client's Product Manager, Architect, QA, and automation functions. Across a legacy estate of 300+ screens, we have released 8 migrated screens to production, with 20 more pending deployment. Successful early results led to extension of the initial five-week Jumpstart through December 2026.

Earlier work includes enterprise architecture for Atlassian, Backbone-to-React modernization and Node.js processing at Trello, Angular Micro Frontends at EPAM, and Visual Basic desktop-to-web modernization at Somer Clinic. My JavaScript/TypeScript and frontend architecture background carries forward into engineering-system ownership, automated verification, and hands-on technical leadership.

Based in Medellin, Colombia. Spanish: native. English: professional working proficiency.
