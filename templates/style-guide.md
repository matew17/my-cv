# ATS Resume Style Guide

- Page size: Letter
- Maximum length: 2 pages
- Layout: single column
- Body font: Arial-compatible sans serif, approximately 9.5-10 pt
- Name: approximately 17-19 pt bold
- Headline: approximately 10.5-11.5 pt bold
- Section headings: approximately 10-11 pt bold, uppercase
- Margins: approximately 0.55-0.65 inches
- No tables for layout
- No sidebars
- No text boxes
- No icons or graphics
- No skill bars
- No photo
- Standard bullet characters only
- Contact links written as parseable text
- Keep dates and roles in normal paragraph flow
- Use PDF and DOCX outputs from the same source to prevent content drift
